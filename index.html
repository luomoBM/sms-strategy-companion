The Santa Magic Sort Strategy Companion

Five short chapters on playing Santa Magic Sort well — a free Christmas liquid sorting game. Verified against the live site on 2026-11-17.

1. The dose is a currency

One tap pair moves one dose, not a layer (rules). Budget doses the way you budget moves in chess: count doses-to-pure before committing to a centrepiece.

2. Shelving is legal

Working bottles may stay mixed all game. Treat them as shelves that each serve one future centrepiece — the seven-step method calls this its first audit.

3. The ladder is the curriculum

Thirty named boards across five pages — from Evergreen Ember through Polar Nightlight — unlock one card at a time on the challenges page. Each card certifies one skill.

4. Restarts are free

No move limit means abandoning a misread board is free (rules). The homepage relaunches any board in one click.

5. The family for afterwards

Liquid Sort Deluxe adds a countdown with opt-outs; Holiday Hex Sort swaps pouring for timed stack-dragging. The comparison page sorts them by pressure.
